How calibration works

The camera needs a known real-world object in the same plane as the ring or finger. This webpage estimates millimeters per pixel from the reference, then converts the detected ring diameter or finger width into ring size.

01

Place reference

Put a debit card, ₹10 coin, ruler mark, or A4 sheet beside the ring or finger.

02

Calibrate scale

The page detects bright rectangular/circular edges or lets you enter pixel width manually.

03

Measure

Align the object inside the guide. The canvas detects edges and converts pixels to millimeters.

Manual Backup Methods

Measure inner ring diameter

Place your ring on a ruler and measure the inside edge to inside edge in millimeters.

Measure finger circumference

Wrap a thin strip of paper around your finger, mark the overlap, then measure the strip length.

Use printable size strip

Print at 100% scale only. Cut the strip, wrap it around your finger, and read the marked size.
